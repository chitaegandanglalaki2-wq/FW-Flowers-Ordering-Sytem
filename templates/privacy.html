Privacy & Cookie Policy

We value your trust and are committed to protecting your personal privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use essential cookies to maintain your account and orders, and how your data is protected.

1. Overview

FuzzyWire Bouquet operates this online store to offer handmade wire bouquets, custom flower arrangements, and gift deliveries throughout Laguna. We handle all customer information transparently and with strict care.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not deploy third-party advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, or behavioral marketing analytics on this website.

2. Essential cookies and session storage

Our website uses strictly essential, functional cookies and local storage required for authentication, account security, and store operations. Specifically:

  • Authentication & Session Management (session cookie): A secure, cryptographically signed HTTP cookie generated by our server. It identifies your active login session as a customer or administrator, preserves your authentication state across pages, and safeguards against session hijacking.
  • CSRF Security Protection (_csrf token): A unique token stored in your session to verify that order placements, profile updates, and payment submissions genuinely come from you, preventing Cross-Site Request Forgery attacks.
  • Shopping Cart & Custom Ordering: Session-based storage to remember your bouquet selections, quantities, and custom flower materials while browsing and proceeding through checkout.
  • Privacy Notice Preference (localStorage): A simple preference stored on your browser when you dismiss the cookie privacy notification, ensuring the banner does not repeatedly appear during your visits.

Because these cookies are strictly necessary to deliver the core service, maintain login status, and process orders securely, the website cannot operate without them. We do not use non-essential or advertising cookies.

3. Information we collect

We only collect information directly submitted by you to fulfill orders and verify accounts:

  • Account Details: Your full name, email address, and an encrypted password hash.
  • Delivery & Contact Information: Street address, barangay, municipality, province (Laguna), and contact telephone number for delivery coordination.
  • Customer Verification Photos: Clear photos of your valid government ID (front and back) and a selfie holding the ID. These photos are stored in a private directory and are only accessible by authorized store administrators to prevent fraudulent orders.
  • Order & Payment Records: Bouquet details, custom flower choices, delivery schedule, and GCash payment reference numbers or proof-of-payment receipts.

4. How we use your information

Your data is used strictly for legitimate store operations:

  • Processing, assembling, and delivering your bouquet orders.
  • Verifying customer accounts and preventing fraud or abuse.
  • Sending one-time verification codes (OTP), order updates, and password recovery emails.
  • Responding to your inquiries and support requests via our customer live chat.

5. Security and data retention

We implement industry-standard safeguards to protect your personal information:

  • Passwords are never stored in plain text; they are encrypted using secure PBKDF2/SHA-256 password hashing.
  • Verification photos are stored in isolated, protected storage outside the public web root.
  • Account verification and password recovery use time-limited one-time passwords (OTP) that expire in 10 minutes.

6. Your rights and contact

You may view or update your profile information at any time through your account settings. If you have questions regarding your data or this policy, please reach out via our on-site live chat or see our Terms and Conditions.
